Our story

"We wanted a room where the coffee is unhurried and the light comes in low."

Tucked into Bishal Chowk on Nakhipot Road, La Semilla started as exactly that — a single seed of an idea: good espresso, honest bakes, and a room worth staying in. The shelves are stacked with pottery instead of props, the plants are here because someone waters them, and the pendant lights stay low and warm past dark.

Today the menu has grown well past the original espresso counter — into iced coffee, frappés, mocktails, lassi, and a bakery case that changes with the day — but the room still runs on the same idea: sit down, take your time, order slowly.
